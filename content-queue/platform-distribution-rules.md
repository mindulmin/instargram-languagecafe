# Language Cafe Platform Distribution Rules

This is the shared pre-publish gate distilled from the supplied Instagram recommendation tips. It is an operational checklist, not a promise of reach.

## Blockers

- Keep Reels under three minutes. Language Cafe uses a thirty-second target.
- Do not use third-party watermarks or reposted/copy content.
- Do not use misleading hooks, forced likes, forced shares, forced comments, or other engagement bait.
- Do not publish content that fails a current Instagram recommendation or community-guideline review.

## Discovery Design

- Carousel: first slide establishes the speaking situation and reason to swipe.
- Carousel visual master: 1080x1920 (9:16) with all essential content inside the centered 1080x1350 safe area. Publish the 1080x1350 feed derivative through the Graph API so Instagram does not crop the teaching copy.
- Carousel captions use natural English first and natural Korean second, covering the same scene and teaching point without literal translation or duplicate hashtag blocks. Hangul remains the lesson core while English makes the context clear worldwide. Do not expose language headings such as `[한국어]`, `[영어]`, `[Korean]`, or `[English]`.
- Carousel images use Hangul only for real dialogue, the target phrase and its Korean components, and limited pronunciation practice. All visible explanations, meaning notes, usage rules, practice directions, swipe reasons, and CTA guidance must be natural English; any other visible Hangul blocks distribution.
- Reel: first three seconds establish a real speaking situation and make the phrase worth learning.
- Use a clear relevant caption. Add topic, location, account tags, or collaboration only when truthful and useful, never as spam.
- Track content over weeks, not single-post follower swings. The three weekly Reel slots create at least ten Reel drafts per month.

## Quality Gate

- Card Carousel Reels: 1080x1920, H.264/yuv420p, 28-32 seconds, and at least 30 fps. The source manifest must list the exact eight approved original cards and confirm no external visual material.
- Music remains an account-owner choice. The automation prepares three mood-and-search intentions, never an assumed song, audio file, or "popular" track.
- Attach the selected Instagram-library track only in the Instagram app immediately before sharing. Never extract, download, scrape, or bake it into the exported Reel.
- When the app exposes a music-volume or mix control, start at 5-10% and review the moving cards with the text visible. If no control is offered or the music reduces readability, share the Reel silent.
- Static carousel music is also app-only and optional. It does not block an otherwise eligible Graph API carousel post.

## Interaction Boundary

- Build captions that invite a real response, but do not demand a like, share, comment, or follow.
- Use comments and direct messages as research for the next expression. Never auto-reply or send unsolicited messages.
- A comment can prioritize only a future ready, non-duplicate expression after two distinct accepted direct requests. A correction or paired-expression suggestion remains editorial evidence unless it passes the same source, readiness, and duplicate gates.
